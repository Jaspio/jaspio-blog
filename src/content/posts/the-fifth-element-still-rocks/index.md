I've just come back from me weekly movie with a big smile on my face(I often stay behind at work to watch movies on the company beamer witha few colleagues ![angel emoticon](http://www.msnemotions.org/emoticons/remix/47.gif)) .

![](http://media.movieweb.com/galleries/1227/622/lo/co5.jpg)

We watched an old campy science fiction flic which I hadn't seen in a long while: [the Fifth Element](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0119116/ "IMDB on the Fifth Element").I had almost forgotten how much I loved the movie: it has great pace,comedy a dash of gratuitous violence but also romance and a moralcritique of where our society is heading.

[more screencaps of the movie](http://www.pcs.ne.jp/%7Eyu/sf/fifth.jpg "more screencaps of the movie")

In short, I was once again thoroughly entertained by this movie.

If you haven't seen it: get thee to a [video store](http://dmoz.org/Home/Consumer_Information/Arts_and_Entertainment/DVD_Rental_Companies/ "DMOZ Video Rental stores") now!
