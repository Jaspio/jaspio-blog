Whilst looking for a weird flag I came across recently (I haven't identified it yet), I touched on this [curious little subsection](http://www.crwflags.com/fotw/flags/jp-.html) of the infamous "Flags of the World" website.

Each prefecture/province has its own flag, as you might expect. But it were the designs that struck me: simple, elegant ([Saitama](http://www.crwflags.com/fotw/flags/jp-11.html)) and many with a rich history (i.e. [Okinawa](http://www.crwflags.com/fotw/flags/jp-47.html)).

A good inspiration for a clan logo? ;)
