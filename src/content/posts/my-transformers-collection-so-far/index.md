I just wanted to share the current status of my Transformers collection.

![&#42; Ricochet (Alternators)&#42; Grimlock (Binaltech)&#42; Skids (Alternators)&#42; Soundwave (G1 original)&#42; Gundam Astray Red Frame (1/44 Bandai model)&#42; Optimus Prime (Alternators)](http://blog.jaspio.net/resserver.php?blogId=2&resource=My%20Transformers%204.jpg)

As many of my friends and some colleagues know, I've recently started collecting new Transformers action figures ever since my interest in Transformers was re-awakened by the upcoming [Transformers: The Movie](http://www.transformersmovie.com/ "website of 'Transformers: The Movie'") directed by [Michael Bay](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Bay "Michael Bay article at Wikipedia").

As of today, I currently own:

1. [Optimus Prime (Masterpiece MP-04 edtion)](http://www.seibertron.com/toys/gallery.php?id=1&size=0&start=0)
2. [Grimlock (Binaltech edition)](http://www.seibertron.com/toys/gallery.php?id=560&size=0&start=0)
3. [Meister (Alternators edition)](http://www.seibertron.com/toys/gallery.php?id=533&size=0&start=0)
4. [Optimus Prime (Alternators edition)](http://www.seibertron.com/toys/gallery.php?id=898&size=0&start=0)
5. [Rollbar (Alternators edition)](http://www.seibertron.com/toys/gallery.php?id=790&size=0&start=0)
6. [Ricochet (Alternators edition)](http://www.seibertron.com/toys/gallery.php?id=789&size=0&start=0)
7. [Skids (Alternators edition)](http://www.seibertron.com/toys/gallery.php?id=840&size=0&start=0)
8. [Tracks (Alternators edition)](http://www.seibertron.com/toys/gallery.php?id=506&size=0&start=0)

These toys for big boys are from from the simple toys of yesteryearbut rather highly detailed, ingeniously constructed pieces ofindustrial art.
