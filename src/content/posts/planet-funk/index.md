Refilling my iPod mini, I came across an album I'd almost forgot about.

[Planet Funk](http://jasper.blogdns.org/www.planetfunkmusic.com "Planet Funk")'s album "[Non Zero Sumness](http://www.allmusic.com/cg/amg.dll?p=amg&token=ADFEAEE47F1FDE4EAC7020CA9D2C54C8B953E00AD70DEE8318374F5792BB3D54871451EC71DFA8BCFDF36AB366ADFF2EA1160ED9CFE95CFDDA765D40&sql=10:26r9286l05ja "Non Zero Sumness on AllMusicGuide.com")" is just one of the best albums in my collection: it's combination of italo, trance and the occasional downbeat lounge-style tracks just blows me away.  
  
My favourite song is "[Chasing the Sun](http://www.planetfunkmusic.com/music/chase_the_sun.rm "listen to Chasing the Sun")", but most people will recognise "[The Switch](http://www.musicstrands.com/action/clip;jsessionid=AE70A1881069B4FF3A8DA0F5EB0CFFB5.srv3?sourceId=&trackId=&type=WMA&clip=us%2Fuswm2%2F566%2F533566_1_11.asx%3Fobj%3Dv41014 "listen to The Switch")", as it was used in a few commercials.

[Listen to it now](http://www.sonymusic.com.au/mediaplayer/home.do?mediaId=510653200010411 "Album preview")!  
You won't regret it.
