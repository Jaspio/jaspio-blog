The incredible blues artist [Seasick Steve](http://www.seasicksteve.com "Official Seasick Steve website") has recently released a ne album entitled "[*I Started Out With Nothin' and I Still Got Most of It Left*](http://www.amazon.co.uk/Started-Nothin-Still-Most-Left/dp/B001D60VTK/ "new album on Amazon")". This album is fantastic, just as great as his debut. This one however has a few more slow and romantic songs on it. My absolute favourite is Walkin' Man.
