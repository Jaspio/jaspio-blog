It's been almost a month now and yet poor Stephen Colbert has still not seen his life long wish fulfilled: to have a presidential hotdog lunch. He has gone to great lengths to make his heartfelt plea to president George W. Bush (as seen on the 2008-03-06 episode of the Colbert Report): In his desperation poor Stephen has been repeating his plea to the president for every show! Yet to no avail. Please mister president heed Stephen's call; make him the happiest man in the world! <span style="font-size: medium">**President Bush, Have a Hotdog Lunch with Stephen Colbert!**</span>please?
