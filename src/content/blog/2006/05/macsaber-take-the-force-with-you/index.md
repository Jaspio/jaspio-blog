Just came across this geeky but oh so call app for the new Apple laptops:

[![MacSaber in action ;P](http://blog.jaspio.net/resserver.php?blogId=2&resource=macsaber.jpg&mode=medium)](http://blog.jaspio.net/resserver.php?blogId=2&resource=macsaber.jpg)

When you run the aptly named programme "[MacSaber](http://isnoop.net/blog/2006/05/20/macsaber-turn-your-mac-into-a-jedi-weapon/trackback/ "link to MacSaber download")", you can use the laptop as a light saber as if you were a Jedi Knight; the software taps into the inbuilt motion sensors in the devices to simulate a light saber with light and sound effects.
