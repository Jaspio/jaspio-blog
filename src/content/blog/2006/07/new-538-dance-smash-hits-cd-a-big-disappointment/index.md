Lately, I haven't been following the popular house music scene as much as I'd like.

So when I recently came across the CD "[Radio 538 Dance Smash Hits 2006.03](http://www.radio538.nl/538/muziek/dancesmashits/index.jsp "Radio 538 Dance Smash")". I thought I'd give it a go.

![38&#95;dance&#95;smash&#95;hits&#95;2006&#95;03 cd cover](http://blog.jaspio.net/resserver.php?blogId=2&resource=538_dance_smash_hits_2006_03.jpg)

After listening to the whole thing I realised I'd missed so frighteningly little. There was just so much crap on this compilation!

Songs like "[From Paris to Berlin](http://system.aim4music.com/shopsupport/download_process/track$prelisten.aspx?media_partner_id=1&bundle_id=43065&sequence_nr=1 "WMA of From Paris to Berlin")" should be banned. Eastern european 18 year-olds calling themselves "Infernal", trying to "sing" in English is just so far removed from cool.

There are a few tasteful tracks on the CD though, most worthy of mention is [BobSinclar](http://bobsinclar.com/index_.html "Bob Sinclar's homepage")'s "[World, Hold On](http://www.vids-c.co.uk/vids/bobsinclar/bobs002.mov "Quicktime - clip from World, Hold On music video")".

Sadly this CD has more sh\*\* than hits on it.  
What a shame.
