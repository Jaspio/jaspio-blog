Yay!

Yesterday, i received my geek box from [BigBadToyStore.com](http://www.bigbadtoystore.com "Big Bad Toy Store").

I ordered a a Transformers [Binaltech](http://www.seibertron.com/toys/index.php?f_series=29 "Binaltech") "[Grimlock](http://www.bigbadtoystore.com/bbts/product.aspx?product=TAK10163&mode=retail&picture=out "Girmlock at BigBadToyStore")" and a new [Gundam](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gundam "Wikipedia on Gundam") model.

The Transformer was especially exciting as this was my first Japanese Binaltech. These are higher quality versions of the American Alternators series. There is a lot less cheap plastic and a lot more diecast metal to play with. It all makes for a much more sturdy and robust action figure.

[![Phonecam shot of Binaltech Grimlock](http://blog.jaspio.net/resserver.php?blogId=2&resource=Image034.jpg&mode=medium)](http://blog.jaspio.net/resserver.php?blogId=2&resource=Image034.jpg)

Although I'm not really a fan of American cars (they're too boxy, brash and "messy" for my liking), this car isn't bad; its nice and shiiinyyy ;)  
Another thing i really liked about this figure is that the head is very much like the original G1 Grimlock head. Ah the memories...

As for the Gundam, I ordered the high quality ["Master Grade" version of the famed F91](http://www.hlj.com/product/BAN945070 "F91 at Hobby Link Japan"). It is a luxury version of the existing model, 1/100 scale, completely buildable without exoskeleton. It has at least ten sheets of parts, so its going to be a long build. Can't wait!
