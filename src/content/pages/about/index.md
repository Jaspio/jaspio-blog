My name is Jasper Rijkeboer.<br />
I am a Microsoft Certified Professional Developer currently working at [Sitecore](https://www.sitecore.com/) Certified Solution [ShareValue](https://www.sharevalue.nl/). Over the past three years I have been lucky enough to work on enterprise-level web applications powered by Sitecore. Before that I have worked in Microsoft based web applications since 2004. In addition to being passionate about development and technology, I am an avid fan of English language stand-up comedy.

I have been using computers since the age of 12 when my dad brought home our very first PC, a 6Mhz [PC XT](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IBM_XT "IBM XT").<br />
The internet sucked me in and assimilated me in 1996 when I plugged in my 14k4 modem. Since then I've been surfing the web, "sharing my opinions" and occasionally work on a website or two.

I've been working with computers professionally since 1996 when I built and sold computers at [Funtronics](https://web.archive.org/web/20000511234754/http://www.funtronics.nl:80/menu.htm) after school and in the weekends. It was there that I helped introduce the first [3dfx Voodoo 3D graphics cards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/3dfx_Interactive#Voodoo_Graphics_PCI) to the Netherlands.
